# Al Baik — Operations Management Case Study

**Type:** Team project (7 members) · **Course:** Operations Management · **Date:** December 2025
**My role:** Author of the **Demand Forecasting** section

## The problem
How does Al Baik, one of Saudi Arabia's most successful quick-service restaurant chains, serve huge crowds quickly, cheaply and with consistent quality — and what should it improve to keep growing?

## What the team covered
Operations strategy · **demand forecasting** · quality management (TQM) · location strategy · inventory management (Just-in-Time) · supply chain management · strategic recommendations. The analysis used market data from Mordor Intelligence (Saudi foodservice market: SAR 113 B in 2025, forecast SAR 167.5 B by 2030).

## My contribution — Demand Forecasting
- Explained how Al Baik uses **short-term forecasting** based on **historical sales, seasonal patterns and daily customer-flow trends** (weekends, holidays, prayer-time rushes).
- Showed how forecasts drive **staff scheduling, cooking cycles and purchasing** of chicken, spices and packaging — preventing shortages at peak hours and avoiding excess inventory.
- Linked forecasting to the **bulk-buying strategy**: knowing demand months ahead lets Al Baik negotiate better supplier prices and stable delivery schedules.
- My section fed directly into the team's inventory (JIT) analysis.

## Team recommendations
1. Expand self-ordering kiosks and app-based ordering to cut queues.
2. Diversify suppliers for non-critical inventory to improve resilience.
3. Build regional central preparation hubs to protect quality during international expansion.
